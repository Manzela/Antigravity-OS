## Becoming a Maintainer

New maintainers are nominated by existing maintainers and approved
by lazy consensus (72-hour window, no objections). Candidates should
demonstrate:

1. Sustained, high-quality contributions over at least 3 months.
2. Deep understanding of the governance kernel architecture.
3. Constructive participation in code reviews and discussions.
4. Alignment with the project's zero-LLM, provider-agnostic philosophy.
